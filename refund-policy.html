Refund Policy

We want every student to feel confident enrolling with Al-Bayan Arabic Isntitute Online. This page explains when refunds are available, how to request one, and how long the process takes.

Eligibility for a Refund

You may request a full refund if:

  • Your class has not yet started, and you cancel at least 48 hours before the first session
  • You were charged more than once for the same registration due to a technical error
  • You paid for a plan or class that we are unable to provide

When Refunds Are Not Available

To keep this fair for all students and instructors, refunds are not issued if:

  • Classes have already started and you have attended one or more sessions
  • The request is made after 7 days from the payment date
  • You simply change your mind after gaining access to class materials or recordings
  • The delay or missed classes are due to circumstances outside our control

How to Request a Refund

Send us a message with your full name, payment reference or transaction ID, and the reason for your request. We'll respond and let you know the outcome within 3–5 business days.

Refund Method & Timing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method used at checkout via Selar, or directly to the account you paid from for manual bank transfers.

Please allow 5–10 business days for the refund to reflect, depending on your bank or card issuer's processing time. This is outside our control once the refund has been initiated on our end.

Plan Changes Instead of a Refund

If you'd prefer to switch plans rather than cancel entirely, let us know — we're happy to adjust your registration and apply any price difference instead of processing a full refund.
