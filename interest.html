Frequently Asked Questions

Are the classes online?

Yes. Al-Bayan Arabic Institute is an online institute, so you can learn from wherever you are.

I am a complete beginner. Can I join?

Yes. We will ask about your current level when we contact you and guide you to the right class.

Do I have to pay to leave my details?

No. Leaving your details is free and does not commit you to anything.

What happens after I submit?

Our admissions team will contact you to answer your questions and help you choose the right level. When you are ready, you can complete the full registration.
